The gateway in action

systemprompt.io is self-hosted AI governance in a single Rust binary. This walkthrough shows the open gateway running end to end: policy checks, audit trails, and cost attribution on every request your team makes.

Why teams switch it on

Your organization is already using AI. The question is whether you can see it, govern it, and keep what it learns. The open gateway puts every request through infrastructure you own.

One gateway, every provider

Anthropic, OpenAI, and Gemini behind a single governed endpoint. Your tools speak one API; the platform owns the keys, the routing, and the policy that sits in front of them.

Every request accounted for

Each inference call and tool invocation lands in an audit trail with a trace linking identity, agent, tool, result, and cost. Usage and spend roll up per user, per agent, per model.

Your data stays yours

One Rust binary and a Postgres database, running on your own machine or your own infrastructure. No SaaS middleman between your prompts and your provider.

Governed by design

Every tool call passes a four-stage governance pipeline — scope check, secret scan, blocklist, rate limit — before it runs. Decisions are audited, not assumed.

Sign in once. Connected to everything.

Create one account on your gateway and every AI tool you use connects through it. The complexity of keys, policy, and audit is owned by the platform, not by you.

AI providers

Anthropic, OpenAI, and Gemini through one governed /v1/messages endpoint, with model routing, cost tracking, and policy enforcement on every call.

MCP servers

Ship your own tools as MCP extensions. Every tool call is scoped, scanned for secrets, and rate-limited before it executes — then audited after.

Claude Cowork & Claude Code

Your skills, agents, and MCP servers arrive inside the Claude tools you already use, installed and kept in sync by the desktop bridge.

Governance spine

Identity, policy evaluations, prompts, responses, and costs land in one Postgres audit table, queryable from the CLI and the analytics dashboard.

How to use it

From your email to your first governed request in a few minutes. No config files, no tokens, no training course.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up with your email — magic-link login, no passwords. You land on the dashboard straight after.

    Sign up Log in
  2. 2

    Download the desktop bridge

    The Systemprompt Bridge runs in your menu bar or system tray. It signs you in, installs your workspace, and keeps your skills in sync inside Claude Code and Claude Cowork.

    Download forMac Download forWindows

    On Linux or an Intel Mac, browse all releases for signed binaries and checksums.

  3. 3

    Connect your AI tools

    Open your dashboard and click Connect to Claude Code in the top-right of any page. One copied command installs your personal marketplace — plugins, skills, agents, and MCP servers, all scoped to your user.

    Open dashboard
  4. 4

    Ask your first question

    Every request now flows through your gateway with policy, audit, and cost attribution built in. Watch it live with "systemprompt infra logs request list", or explore the analytics dashboard.
